# Caribou Technologies: Company Overview

There are two major, distinct entities recognized globally as **Caribou Technologies**. Depending on your industry of focus, they refer either to an **IoT and enterprise software provider** or a **medical device contract manufacturer**. Both overviews are detailed below in Markdown format.

---

## 1. Caribou Technologies Incorporated (IoT & Software Services)

**Caribou Technologies Incorporated** is a privately held IT consulting and services company that specializes in building end-to-end Internet of Things (IoT) solutions and managing enterprise software environments. 

### Key Information
* **Founded:** 2012
* **Headquarters:** Toronto, Canada
* **Global Offices:** Hyderabad, India (est. 2015)
* **Core Focus:** IoT engineering, Android enterprise software, and cloud management platforms.

### Core Offerings & Specialties
* **IoT Infrastructure:** End-to-end deployment including cloud software, mobile applications, embedded firmware, and hardware integration.
* **Android Enterprise & MDM:** Developing custom Android OS builds and implementing Mobile Device Management (MDM) solutions to simplify deploying devices into managed environments like education, healthcare, and retail.
* **Production Operations:** Offering ongoing support, quality assurance (QA) testing services, and maintaining enterprise solutions with industry-leading uptime.

---

## 2. Caribou Technologies (Medical Device Manufacturing)

**Caribou Technologies** is a premier medical device contract manufacturer specializing in precision micro-manufacturing for complex components. Note that this entity was acquired by Resonetics in February 2019 to expand precision micro-manufacturing capabilities.

### Key Information
* **Founded:** 2005
* **Headquarters:** Blaine, Minnesota, United States
* **Parent Company:** Resonetics (Acquired via private equity firm GTCR)
* **Core Focus:** Medical equipment and component fabrication.

### Manufacturing Capabilities
* **Technical Specialties:** Expert capabilities in centerless grinding, forming, coiling, machining, tube processing, and wire processing.
* **Production Spectrum:** Partners with healthcare clients from rapid prototyping and R&D stages up through high-volume production lines.
* **Target Applications:** Primarily manufactures componentry for vascular intervention, minimally invasive surgery, and electrophysiology applications.
